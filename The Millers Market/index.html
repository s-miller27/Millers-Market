Our Story

Founded on a love for the timeless and the forgotten, The Millers Market is a family-run collection of vintage glassware and unique treasures. What started as a weekend passion — wandering estate sales and flea markets — has grown into a carefully curated shop where every piece has a story.

We believe the best things were built to last. From Depression-era glass to Mid-Century barware, each item in our collection is hand-selected for its craftsmanship, character, and enduring beauty. Because every treasure deserves a second chance to be cherished.

When we're not hunting for the next great find, you'll find us at home in the Midwest with our two kids, a very opinionated cat, and an ever-growing collection of our own.

— The Miller Family
